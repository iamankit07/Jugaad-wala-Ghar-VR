# Low Poly Garage Props Pack

Thank you for downloading the Low Poly Garage Props Pack! 
This package contains a collection of stylized, low-poly assets designed to quickly fill out garage environments, workshops, or level backgrounds. Perfect for indie games, mechanic simulators, or survival titles.

## Contents
The package includes 17 unique prefabs ready to be dragged and dropped into your scene:
* Storage shelf & Workbench (plus assembled variants)
* Barrels & Wooden pallet
* Car wheel & Car battery
* Jerrycans (Large and Small)
* Tools: Wrenches (3 sizes), Screwdriver, Hammer, Bolts
* Trash can

## Technical Details
* Number of unique models: 17
* Polygon count: ~3,500 triangles total (average under 500 per model)
* Textures: 1 shared color palette texture (PNG, 256x256) for maximum optimization
* Materials: 2 simple materials
* Collision: Yes (Custom Box Colliders included on all prefabs)
* LODs: Not included
* Render Pipelines: Fully compatible with Built-in and URP

## How to Use
1. Open the "Prefabs" folder.
2. Drag and drop any prefab directly into your scene.
3. All models share a single texture palette, meaning you can easily modify colors by editing the provided texture file or adjusting the material.
